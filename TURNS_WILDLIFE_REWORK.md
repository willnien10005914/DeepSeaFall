# Turning, shark contact and new wildlife — release 37

Large directional reversals trigger a 2.6-second axial pirouette or a 3-second forward/backward somersault. The sequence eases through a full rotation while the waist curves toward the new direction and the knees hook/fold. Torso sway also follows smaller steering changes. Movement slows in the middle of a turn. A short cooldown prevents repeated spins during one gesture, and idle/sleep/restart transitions preserve finite poses. These are real-time skeletal animations layered onto the existing swim clips, not a newly trained policy.

Girl/shark contact now uses nineteen posed girl body/extremity spheres against shark trunk, tail and fin capsules, with a 0.025 m margin. All nearby sharks are resolved together in the playable XY plane. Local projection handles ordinary contacts; a shortest clear planar escape search handles deep overlapping/concave capsule unions. Body contact nudges without applying bite damage; existing swept-mouth bite rules and whale scare behavior remain. These conservative proxies are gameplay collision, not exact mesh or fluid simulation.

New 3D visitors: an orange/white clownfish with edged translucent fins and fin rays; reflective silver cutlassfish with a long dorsal membrane; a banded swimming sea snake; and a large fictional kraken with eight deforming, tapered tentacles and 96 suction cups. Clownfish appear at the beginning, and rotating visitors introduce the other species over time. Hazardous species can damage on contact with cooldowns and are recognized by the existing avoidance policy. Their geometry/materials are procedural; no claim of scanned or photoreal assets is made.

Validation includes automatic reversal activation and completion, bounded frame-to-frame rotation, nine turn/garment representative pose audits, eight shark orientations, two overlapping sharks together, preserved depth plane, sea-snake damage cooldown, rendered examples of all four species, and live game reversal/spawning. Tap swimming, bite/gem/sleep/reset and whale/shark fright regressions are retained. Representative checks do not guarantee zero garment intersections for all poses. Reports: assets/previews/v37.
