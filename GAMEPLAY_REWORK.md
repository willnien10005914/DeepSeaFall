# Revision 31: ingots, hazard feedback and staged swim propulsion

Gold octahedra are replaced with bowl-shaped 3D ingots. Each collectible restores 20 stamina, capped at 100, and increments the ingot counter exactly once. Ten pickup sparks and an ingot fly from the projected pickup position toward STAMINA; landing pulses the meter and shows the actual credited amount. Every fifth ingot also grants seaweed for the existing revive mechanic. Pickups no longer randomly change the character appearance. They spawn near the reachable Z plane; a welcome ingot provides an early encounter.

Rock contacts use seven live body spheres, a world-space box broad phase and BVH queries against visible rock triangles. They cover spawned hazards and the canyon/foreground rock meshes. Open cliff surfaces use unsigned proximity; closed rocks use an outward ray test for inside contacts. Penetration is separated, an impact deducts 10 stamina (14 for volcanic hazards), a red edge flash and damage label appear, and recoil plus a 0.95-second invulnerability interval prevent damage every frame. Whale protection retains its damage reduction. Distant rocks do not collide solely because they overlap on screen.

The kinematic swim controller caps normal ascent/descent at 0.46/0.54 m/s with 0.80 m/s² acceleration. A 2.05-second gather / reach / kick / glide cycle delays the strong push until after leg gathering and arm extension. Alternating leg/ankle motion follows the push; head-first and upward orientation blend gradually. Release and reversal brake smoothly. Environmental descent is slower too. This is authored clip blending plus procedural strokes, not fluid-dynamics training. Existing hair and cloth contact remain enabled; their earlier high computation cost is not solved by this revision.

Validation includes a deterministic six-second controller/reversal/rest test, real game pickup and capped/duplicate reward checks, HUD arrival, spawned/background/far-rock collision and damage cooldown checks, restart cleanup, and eight up/down stage poses with cloth intersection audits. Final installed verification exercises the same game methods without an exhaustive long-run performance claim.
